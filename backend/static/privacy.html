  • Account (username, avatar, email and hashed password, or Apple/Google ID) — to run your account (contract).

SKILKI is for people aged 13 and over. Under 15, only non-personalised ads are shown.

Access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability, and withdrawal of consent at any time (Settings → "Manage privacy choices" for ads).
